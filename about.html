About us

Singapore's award winning designers.

Our passion

CREATE is a community of passionate & creative designers & planners from Singapore, Malaysia & the Philippines, with a vision unto the future & beyond the shores of the little red dot.

Since inception, we have garnered much attention for design excellence from Singapore's main newspaper The Straits Times, top interior design magazines Home & Décor, Designaire and Home + Living, and leading design portals Qanvast & Houzz.

Ethos

We craft spaces, forms & experiences that reflect each client's passion & aspirations.

CREATE was formed by Rick as an integrated spatial-design practice. Our design process unfolds in a democratic & explorative environment between our partners, clients & craftsmen, before being integrated into form, site, object and space.

We begin every project upstream. Before a single material is chosen we look for a central idea, a north star, strong enough to guide the whole project, then translate it through planning, joinery, lighting, materials and styling so that each home or space becomes a world of its own.

“A home should feel like it was always meant to be this way: considered, calm and unmistakably yours.”

Rick, Principal Designer, CREATE

Our designers
Rick
Principal Designer

Rick is an experienced, multifaceted designer with over ten years in the industry, ranging from product design to his current focus, interior design.

He serves as Principal Designer of CREATE Interior Design Co., leading every project from first conversation to final styling.

Our studio

CREATE Interior Design Studio is a Singapore-based design atelier known for crafting refined, meaningful spaces that balance aesthetic elegance with thoughtful functionality. Rooted in a passion for world-class design and a keen understanding of modern living, CREATE transforms lifestyle aspirations into environments that resonate with personality, harmony and purpose.

With a collaborative spirit and a commitment to excellence, the studio brings bespoke creativity to every project, from residential sanctuaries to lifestyle-driven concepts, elevating the everyday into the exceptional.
