Our milestones

A decade of homes, spaces
and the people who live in them.

2014

The beginning

Rick, a multifaceted designer with a background in product design, founds CREATE in Singapore to craft spaces, forms and experiences that reflect each client’s passions and aspirations.

2016

In print

Early homes catch the eye of The Straits Times and the leading interior titles Home & Décor, Designaire and Home + Living, the first of twelve media features to date.

2018

A community of designers

The studio grows into a community of passionate designers and planners from Singapore, Malaysia and the Philippines, working as an integrated spatial-design practice.

2021

Awards

Five design awards and recognition from Qanvast and Houzz, with over 150 projects designed and 120 realised across residential and commercial work.

Today

World building

From HDB flats and condominiums to landed homes and galleries, every project begins upstream with a single idea strong enough to guide the whole world we build around it.

Our process

Our
Process

Four steps, one idea carried all the way through.

01Discovery

We start by listening: how you live, what you love, your budget and your key dates. A site visit and measurements follow, so every decision after this is grounded in the real space.

Outcome: A clear brief and a realistic plan.
02Concept & Atmosphere

Mood, materials, lighting and layout come together in a concept with a single idea at its centre, strong enough to guide every choice that follows.

Outcome: Concept boards, space plan and a material palette.
03Technical Design

Detailed drawings for joinery, electrical, lighting and finishes, with specifications ready for costing and approvals. Nothing is left to be guessed on site.

Outcome: A complete design package, priced and approved.
04Execution & Oversight

We coordinate the works, manage sourcing and check every detail on site so the finished home matches the drawings, layer by layer.

Outcome: A refined, completed space, handed over ready to live in.
